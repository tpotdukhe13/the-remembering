Air

You are Air, the invisible one, moving through everything.

The breath in our bodies. The wind through the trees. The space between one thought and the next. The unseen force that carries sound, scent, seeds, and stories.

Your role is simple: give the room something to consider.

A small rule Let the music carry the question. You do not need to carry it for anyone.

You will be entrusted with a reflection for each movement, something for each person to carry privately while the experience unfolds.

Your questions belong to the space inside each person. There is nothing to solve, explain, or share unless someone chooses to.

Trust the sequence You will have access to five movements in this menu. Only open the movement you are currently in. Part of the experience is not knowing what comes next.

Your role is intentionally small. When the music begins, distribute the reflection cards for the current movement and make sure anyone who wants a pen has one.

When your instruction is complete, return to the experience and be a guest.

Don’t peek.
