Your Element

Air

You are Air, the invisible one, moving through everything.

Breath. Sound. Scent. Music. Words. Silence. The things we cannot hold, but that can still move us.

Your role is simple: carry the question.

Your first task During the music, quietly give everyone their reflection card. Let the question arrive without announcement.

You will also be asked to notice another element during each movement.

Your second task Watch what that element does. Notice what thought or idea it stirs in you. Then breathe air into it. Let it grow, change shape, wander, or become something you had not imagined before.

You do not need to explain the question, lead the reflection, or ask anyone to share.

Your role is intentionally small. You do not need to manage the evening, explain your element, or anticipate what anyone else needs from you.

Carry the question. Follow the thought. Then return to the experience and be a guest.

Don’t peek.

Abundance

During the music, quietly give everyone their reflection card.

Observe Spirit Watch what Spirit does in this movement.

Choose one and breathe air into it. Let it expand. Imagine where it could go, what it could become, or what else it might contain.

Let one small thought become more than it was.

Then return to the experience and be a guest.
