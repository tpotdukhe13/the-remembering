Your Element

Air

You are Air, the invisible one, moving through everything.

Breath. Sound. Scent. Music. Words. Silence. The things we cannot hold, but that can still move us.

Your role is simple: carry the question.

A small rule You do not need to explain the question, lead the reflection, or ask anyone to share.

You will be entrusted with a paper fan and reflection cards for the evening.

During each musical piece, quietly give everyone their reflection card. Let the question arrive without announcement.

Then observe Notice what happens after the question enters the room. You are not looking for answers. Notice what moves, what settles, what changes, and what remains.

Your role is intentionally small. You do not need to manage the evening, explain your element, or anticipate what anyone else needs from you.

Carry the question. Then return to the experience and be a guest.

Don’t peek.

Movement I

Abundance

During the music, quietly give everyone their reflection card.

The question When have you felt yourself held by something you did not create?

Do not explain the question or ask anyone to answer aloud.

Let the question move through the room.

Notice what sustains the room before anyone names it.

Then return to the experience and be a guest.

Movement II

Imagination

During the music, quietly give everyone their reflection card.

The question What would you imagine if you knew your life was part of something still becoming?

Do not explain the question or ask anyone to answer aloud.

Let the question have room to wander.

Notice what becomes possible when something is still unfinished.

Then return to the experience and be a guest.

Movement III

Connection

During the music, quietly give everyone their reflection card.

The question Where do you feel you belong simply by being part of something larger than yourself?

Do not explain the question or ask anyone to answer aloud.

As the meal comes together, notice what moves between people.

Notice how separate actions become one thing.

Then return to the experience and be a guest.

Movement IV

Fracture

During the music, quietly give everyone their reflection card.

The question Where have you mistaken separation for freedom?

Do not explain the question or ask anyone to answer aloud.

Let the question remain in the room, even if the room becomes uncomfortable.

Notice what happens when connection begins to break apart.

Then return to the experience and be a guest.

Movement V

Return

During the music, quietly give everyone their reflection card.

The question How might your body know that you are remembering before your mind does?

Do not explain the question or ask anyone to answer aloud.

Notice what remains after the story. Breath. Bodies. Warmth. Silence. Whatever the evening has left behind.

Notice what the body knows before the mind finds words for it.

Then return to the experience and be a guest.
