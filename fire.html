Your Element

Fire

You are Fire, the force that changes what it touches.

Heat. Light. Transformation. Destruction. Renewal. The spark that gathers people close.

Your role is simple: tend to the transformation.

A small rule
Notice what changes when something is offered to fire. You do not need to explain the change. Let it be witnessed.

You will be entrusted with fire for the evening.

Your work will move through five forms: offering, arranging, lighting, marking, and transforming.

Before we begin, spend five minutes in the kitchen to clarify your role and responsibilities for the experience. Once we begin, trust the sequence. Your instructions will be waiting when you need them.

Trust the sequence

You will have access to five movements in this menu. Only open the movement you are currently in. Part of the experience is not knowing what comes next.

Before each movement begins, open the instructions. Read them before the movement starts so you know what to notice, what to do, and when to act. Then return to the experience.

Your role is intentionally small. You do not need to manage the evening, explain your element, or anticipate what anyone else needs from you.

When your instruction is complete, return to the experience and be a guest.

Don’t peek.

Movement I

Abundance

At the end of the meal, but before the next movement, offer everyone a choice of incense.

Let each person choose the scent they are drawn toward.

As you offer
Do not explain the choices. Let the choosing belong to them.

Once everyone has chosen, collect the incense and return to being a guest.

Notice how many different ways abundance can smell.

Movement II

Imagination

At any point during this movement, arrange the chosen incense in the various gold holders and arrange around the rooms.

As you arrange
Let the room become part of the experience. Place each scent where it can be encountered rather than simply seen.

Do NOT light them yet.

Some things are imagined before they are ignited.

Movement III

Connection

Before movement begins and the bell is rung, light all of the incense you arranged around the rooms.

Move quietly and allow the scents to begin spreading before everyone notices what has changed.

As you light
Notice how one small flame can alter an entire space without touching it.

Once everything is lit, return to your place before the story begins.

Fire travels. What begins in one place does not necessarily remain there.

Movement IV

Fracture

When the music starts to play, use fallen ash from the incense to mark each person.

Move from person to person quietly.

Marking
Place a small X with the ash on each person. Let the mark remain visible.

Do not explain what the mark means.

What fire transforms does not always return unchanged.

When everyone has been marked, return to your place and let the music continue.

Movement V

Return

At any point during this movement, retrieve the ends of the incense sticks and keep them with you.

Transform
Remember what has already been burned, changed, and spent, can create something that will be received.

Fire changes. Fire leaves something behind. Fire can begin again.

You were a transformative example of Fire tonight. Thank you for changing us.
