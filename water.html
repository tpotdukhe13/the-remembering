Your Element

Water

You are Water, the one that receives.

You move through everything. You gather, carry, nourish, reflect, and return.

Your role
Listen. Notice what is offered. When the moment comes, let something go a little deeper.

You will be entrusted with a rainstick for the evening. Turn it slowly when it is time. You will then ask everyone a discussion question.

Additionally, at certain moments, you will be asked to listen closely to someone else's experience. When the moment feels right, ask that person one genuine follow-up question. You will know who.

Your task

You are given a question to reveal for each movement. Do so only once the meal is served. Wait for everyone to take a few bites, and then ask the question.

Let the conversation continue naturally. You do not need to manage it, explain it, or keep it going.

Before we begin, spend five minutes in the kitchen to clarify your role and responsibilities for the evening. Once we begin, trust the sequence. Your instructions will be waiting when you need them.

Trust the sequence You will have access to five movements in this menu. Only open the movement you are currently in. Part of the experience is not knowing what comes next.

Before each movement begins, open the respective instructions. Read them before the movement starts so you know what to notice, what to do, and when to act. Reveal the question only when the time arrives.
Receive first. Respond second. Then return to being a guest.

Don’t peek.
