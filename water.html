Your Element

Water

You are Water, the one that receives.

You move through everything. You gather, carry, nourish, reflect, and return.

Your role Listen. Notice what is offered. When the moment comes, let something go a little deeper.

You will be entrusted with a rainstick for the evening. Turn it slowly when your time arrives.

At certain moments, you will also be asked to listen closely to someone else's experience.

A small rule You are given a question to ask in each movement, you can do so when a few bites have been taken. Turn your rainstick as slow as you can, you may reveal your question in the appropriate movement at that time only, and then ask it to the group.

You will also be entrusted in each movement to listen to a different element. When the moment feels right, ask that person one genuine follow-up question.

Let the conversation continue naturally. You do not need to manage it, explain it, or keep it going.

Trust the sequence You will have access to five movements in this menu. Only open the movement you are currently in. Part of the experience is not knowing what comes next.
Receive first. Respond second. Then return to being a guest.

Don’t peek.
