Your Element
            

Water

        
        
            

You are Water, the one that moves between things.

            

You reflect. You carry. You connect. You find the places beneath the surface.

            

Your role is simple: listen, and let something go a little deeper.

            
                Your task                 In each movement, you will be asked to listen especially closely to one other element.             
            

When that element speaks, acts, or offers something to the room, pay attention.

            

If a moment presents itself, ask that person one conversational follow-up question. Let the question come from what they have actually said or done.

            
                Nothing scripted                 You are not given a question to ask. You are given someone to listen to.             
            

You do not need to force the moment. You do not need to make the conversation deeper than it naturally wants to go.

            
                Trust the sequence                 You will have access to five movements in this menu. Only open the movement you are currently in. Part of the experience is not knowing what comes next.             
            
                Listen closely. Ask once. Then let the conversation flow.             
            

Don’t peek.
