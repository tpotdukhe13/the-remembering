Your Element

Earth

You are Earth, the solid one, the place where everything lands.

The soil beneath our feet. The force of gravity. The food in our mouths. The weight of a stone in our hands.

Your role is simple: ground the room.

A small rule When the experience gets too far into the clouds, bring it gently back to the ground.

You will be entrusted with a stone for the evening.

Whenever you place the stone at the center of the table, it is an invitation for everyone to take a collective breath.

Trust the sequence You will have access to five movements in this menu. Only open the movement you are currently in. Part of the experience is not knowing what comes next.

Your role is intentionally small. You do not need to manage the evening, explain your element, or anticipate what anyone else needs from you. Simply stay grounded in yourself and remain aware of how you are helping to create a sense of grounding for everyone.

When your instruction is complete, return to the experience and be a guest.

Don’t peek.

Movement I

Abundance

When Nature finishes the story, place the stone at the center of the table.

Then say “Whenever the stone is placed, it is an invitation for us to breathe together.”

Invite everyone to take one collective breath.

Then return to being a guest.

Stay grounded in your own body. Notice the room around you. If another breath ever feels needed, offer one collectively.

Movement II

Imagination

When Nature finishes the story, place the stone at the center of the table.

Bring your thumb and index finger together.

Invite everyone to do the same.

Take one collective breath.

Let the breath ground whatever the imagination has opened. Keep yourself grounded. Notice what is happening around you. A quiet moment of eye contact can be just as grounding.
Movement III

Connection

When Nature finishes the story, place the stone at the center of the table.

Bring your thumb and middle finger together.

Invite everyone to do the same.

Take one collective breath.

Consider How does connection affect our ability to stay grounded? Let your presence be a steady one.
Movement IV

Fracture

When Nature finishes the story, place the stone at the center of the table.

Bring your thumb and ring finger together.

Invite everyone to do the same.

Take one collective breath.

Let the silence exist. If you sense unsteadiness, lead another collective breath without saying a word.
Movement V

Return

When Nature finishes the story, place the stone at the center of the table.

Bring your thumb and pinky finger together.

Invite everyone to do the same.

Take one collective breath.

Return Bring your attention back to your body. Your seat. Your feet. The ground beneath you. Your breath.

Notice the others around you. Notice their energies. Allow for everyone to be held all at once.
